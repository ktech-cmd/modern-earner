🔒 Privacy Policy

Your Privacy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Information We Collect

Earner Tools does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. All calculator inputs you enter are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

2. Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (pages visited, country of visitor, device type). This data is anonymous and does not identify you personally. We may also display advertisements via Google AdSense, which uses cookies to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalised ads via Google Ad Settings.

3. Third-Party APIs

Our cryptocurrency converter uses the CoinGecko public API to fetch live exchange rates. This request is made from your browser directly to CoinGecko. We do not log or store the rates fetched. Please review CoinGecko's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data at risk. All pages are served over HTTPS.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at earnertools@outlook.com.
