🔒 Privacy & Compliance

Privacy Policy

How we handle data, maintain security, and respect your privacy.

1. Introduction & Overview

At Earner Tools (accessible via earnertools.co.ke), we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of user privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Earner Tools and how we use and protect it.

2. Client-Side Processing & Financial Data Confidentiality

Our primary principle is Privacy by Design. All financial calculations—including your gross revenues, target income, tax brackets, hourly rates, social media metrics, and cryptocurrency transactions—are processed 100% locally within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not store, harvest, transmit, or retain your financial figures on our servers.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Earner Tools uses standard cookies and browser storage (such as localStorage) to store your aesthetic preferences (for example, keeping your choice between Light Mode and Dark Mode across page navigations). These functional cookies do not contain any personal identification or tracking data.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We partner with third-party advertising platforms, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to Earner Tools or other websites on the Internet:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the World Wide Web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info or Network Advertising Initiative (NAI).

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Earner Tools, which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Earner Tools has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

5. Google Analytics & Aggregate Traffic Data

We use Google Analytics to monitor anonymous aggregate traffic patterns, including general geographic distribution, browser and device categories, and page view counts. This data assists us in optimizing mobile performance, identifying tool demand, and refining calculation formulas. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and reports website trends without identifying individual visitors.

6. Third-Party APIs (CoinGecko)

Our USDT to KES currency conversion tool queries the public API provided by CoinGecko to provide live exchange pricing. This request is dispatched directly from your browser client to CoinGecko's secure API endpoint. No user-identifying data is attached to this request. For further details, please consult CoinGecko's Privacy Policy.

7. GDPR, CCPA & Kenya Data Protection Compliance

Under data protection regulations—including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA) 2019—users are entitled to specific rights regarding their information:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You have the right to request the correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You have the right to direct businesses not to sell or share your personal data. (Earner Tools does not sell any personal data).

8. Children's Information

Protecting the privacy of young children on the internet is especially important. Earner Tools does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.

9. Consent & Policy Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect evolving regulatory standards and website features.

10. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by email at earnertools@outlook.com.
