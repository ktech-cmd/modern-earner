Our Mission

Earner Tools builds free, financial calculators for freelancers, content creators, and digital earners — especially those in Kenya and across Africa who are chronically underserved by mainstream finance tools.

Most financial calculators are built for salaried employees in Western markets. They don't account for platform fees, local tax systems like KRA, or the reality of getting paid in USD and spending in KES.

We're changing that — one tool at a time.

Data & Accuracy

All tax data is sourced directly from official government sources (KRA, KNBS, World Bank). We update our figures after every Finance Act and display a "last verified" date on each tool.

Our tools are for guidance only and should not be used as a substitute for professional tax or financial advice. Always verify with a licensed professional before filing.
