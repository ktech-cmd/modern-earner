Our Mission

Earner Tools was created with a clear objective: to provide free, transparent, and accurate financial tools for freelancers, digital creators, remote contractors, and independent professionals—especially across Africa and emerging markets.

Traditional financial calculators assume a standard corporate 9-to-5 paycheck with direct domestic deductions. They fail to account for the unique financial complexities modern earners navigate every single day: tiered platform commissions (Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal), multi-currency conversions (USD to KES), mobile money withdrawal bands (M-Pesa), dynamic YouTube RPM geography tiers, and local tax legislation such as the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) Finance Act.

We bridge this information gap by delivering fast, intuitive, and accurate calculators that reflect how you actually work and get paid.

What We Build
  • Freelance Take-Home Calculator: Calculates exact net take-home pay after deducting platform service fees and graduated KRA income tax brackets with monthly personal relief.
  • Freelancer Rate Calculator: Reverse-engineers your annual or monthly income target into precise hourly, daily, and project rates—accounting for non-billable overhead, vacation buffer, and taxes.
  • YouTube & AdSense Estimator: Projects monthly and annual ad revenues based on 15 content niches and audience geographic distribution multipliers (updated for 2025/2026).
  • Brand Deal Rate Calculator: Establishes fair pricing benchmarks for sponsored reels, TikToks, YouTube integrations, and retainers using engagement rate and audience demographics.
  • USDT → KES Crypto Converter: Converts digital stablecoins to Kenyan Shillings with live CoinGecko rates, P2P spreads, and official M-Pesa withdrawal fee tiers.
Data Methodology & Transparency

We take data integrity seriously. Our calculation models are rigorously maintained and cross-referenced with primary authoritative sources:

  • Taxation: Directly mapped to official Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) PAYE brackets and legislative schedules, including Finance Act 2026.
  • Exchange Rates: Connected via public API endpoints to CoinGecko for live crypto valuation, complemented by bank and P2P spread modeling.
  • Payment & Mobile Money Fees: Sourced from published Safaricom M-Pesa tariff schedules and official platform terms (Upwork, Fiverr, Toptal).
  • Creator Benchmarks: Synthesized from global influencer and creator economy industry reports (CreatorIQ, Influencer Marketing Hub, YouTube partner analytics).
